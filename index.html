Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I plan a trip on Wayvora?

You can create a new trip by clicking "Plan a Trip" in the navigation bar or selecting "Add to Trip" on any destination card. Enter your travel dates and start arranging attractions day by day!

Are my saved favourites and itineraries kept when I refresh?

Yes! Wayvora automatically saves your favourites and planned itineraries to your browser's local storage, ensuring your plans stay right where you left them.

Can I print or export my itinerary?

Absolutely. Inside the Trip Planner, simply click the "Print / Export" button to generate a clean, distraction-free printable travel summary.

How are the daily budgets calculated?

Our budget breakdowns reflect average mid-range travel estimates including accommodations, meals, local transit, and popular attraction admissions.
